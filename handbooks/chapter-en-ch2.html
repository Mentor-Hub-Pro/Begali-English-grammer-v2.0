Quick method: Rule → Bengali meaning → Example → Own sentence → MCQ check.

1. Full Detailed Theory

Learning goal: Choose the correct tense from time, completion and duration clues.

  1. First choose Present/Past/Future; then choose Simple/Continuous/Perfect/Perfect Continuous.
  2. Simple = usual/fact; Continuous = in progress; Perfect = completed/result; Perfect Continuous = duration.
  3. Helping verbs reveal tense quickly.
  4. Tense choice depends on meaning first; signal words support the choice but never replace understanding.
  5. Stative verbs such as know, believe, own and understand are usually not used in continuous form.
  6. Perfect tenses connect one time with another: result now, earlier past, or completion by a future point.
