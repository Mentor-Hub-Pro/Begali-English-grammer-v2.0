1. Full Detailed Theory

Learning goal: Use a, an, the or zero article by sound and meaning.

  1. a/an introduce one non-specific countable noun; the points to a specific/known noun.
  2. Use an before vowel sound, not merely vowel letter.
  3. Zero article is common with languages, subjects, meals and plural/material nouns in general sense.
  4. Article choice comes before a singular countable noun; plural and uncountable nouns follow different rules.
  5. The is not simply “a known noun”; it is also used with unique items, groups, superlatives and certain geographical names.
  6. Pronunciation decides a/an: /juː/ in university starts with a consonant sound.

Article-এর মূল কথা: নতুন, চেনা, না-লাগা

Article হল noun-এর ছোট পরিচয়পত্র। a/an = একটি অনির্দিষ্ট বা নতুন জিনিস; the = নির্দিষ্ট/আগে চেনা জিনিস; zero article = কিছু noun-এর আগে কিছুই বসে না।
