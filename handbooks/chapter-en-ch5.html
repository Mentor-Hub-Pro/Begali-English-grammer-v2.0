1. Full Detailed Theory

Learning goal: Report statements, questions, commands and wishes logically.

  1. Statement: said/told + that; yes/no question: asked + if/whether; WH-question keeps the WH-word.
  2. Imperative: told/requested/advised + object + to + V1.
  3. Change tense, pronoun, time and place according to the reporting situation.
  4. Backshift is common when the reporting verb is in the past, but universal truths and still-true facts may stay unchanged.
  5. Pronoun change follows speaker/listener meaning, not a fixed mechanical replacement.
  6. Reporting verb selection carries meaning: requested, advised, ordered, forbade, exclaimed and wished are not interchangeable.

Narration: tense, pronoun and expression change

Direct speech gives the exact words in quotation marks. Indirect speech reports the meaning without quotation marks. Change reporting verb, pronoun, tense and time/place words logically.

Questions, commands, exclamations and wishes

Sentence type changes the reporting verb and connector. Do not use that in an indirect question; do not retain question order.

Yes/No interrogative: 15 examples

Use asked + if/whether. Change question order to subject + verb.

  1. He said to me, “Are you ready?” → He asked me if I was ready.
  2. Rina said, “Do you read daily?” → Rina asked if I read daily.
  3. Mother said, “Have you eaten?” → Mother asked if I had eaten.
  4. The teacher said, “Can you solve it?” → The teacher asked if I could solve it.
  5. He said, “Will you come?” → He asked if I would come.
  6. She said, “Did you see him?” → She asked if I had seen him.
  7. Father said, “Is the door open?” → Father asked if the door was open.
  8. They said, “Are we late?” → They asked if they were late.
  9. I said, “May I enter?” → I asked if I might enter.
  10. The officer said, “Do you know the rule?” → The officer asked if I knew the rule.
  11. He said, “Were you sleeping?” → He asked if I had been sleeping.
  12. She said, “Has Rina arrived?” → She asked if Rina had arrived.
  13. Mother said, “Will it rain?” → Mother asked if it would rain.
  14. The boy said, “Can I play?” → The boy asked if he could play.
  15. Teacher said, “Have they finished?” → The teacher asked if they had finished.

Study flow: Sentence type → Reporting verb → Pronoun → Tense/time/place change
