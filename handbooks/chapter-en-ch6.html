1. Full Detailed Theory

Learning goal: Build high-frequency vocabulary by meaning families and usage.

  1. Synonym means similar; antonym means opposite; one-word substitution replaces a long clue.
  2. Idioms and phrasal verbs must be learned as complete units.
  3. Prefix/suffix and word family help in fill-in-the-blank questions.
  4. Vocabulary retention improves when you meet a word in a sentence, retrieve it later, and use it actively.
  5. Confusable words are often separated by word class, spelling pattern and one contrast sentence.
  6. Phrasal verbs can be literal or idiomatic; sentence context tells which meaning is intended.
